## 0.2.0-rc.2 (2026-10-08)

- Scrolling is no longer sluggish while the model is replying; smoothness now matches the official dark and light themes, including while the model is thinking.
- Fixed the tone jumping back to an earlier pick when you switch between tones quickly.
- Version-line alignment with official dsh 0.2.0-rc.2.
- Fixed grouped headings in the model picker and command popup losing their themed background.
- Fixed the right sidebar's themed gradient being offset on the Windows desktop app.
- New: with a tone picked, narrow windows keep the turn navigation, and content gets more side margin.
- The resize drag handle's highlight now follows the pointer, even with no tone picked.
- Fixed a washed-out band on the right of the conversation after opening the right sidebar.
- Fixed the right sidebar's file preview not scrolling.
- Restored the texture and lighting on question cards, plan-review cards, the archive page and the cloud-files page, plus the blurred background behind the last two.
- Restored the blurred background behind modal dialogs.
- Fixed the dark band on a stuck "Think" row and the background band on grouped headings.
- Fixed the top bar's lighting being blocked, and uneven lighting across pop-ups.
- Fixed the translucency of the composer and top bar in both themes, and the drag handle overlapping the top bar.
- Fixed conversation text showing through the composer card's rounded corners.
- Fixed the Windows title bar not matching the themed sidebar below it.
- Fixed the right sidebar's top rows not matching the conversation header's appearance.
- Fixed the right sidebar's scrollbar scrolling up into the top rows.
- Fixed the background-jobs popover being cut off by the right sidebar.
- Fixed the trajectory page's colour stopping short of the input box.
- Brightened the top highlights in the dark tones.
- Brightened the lighting on the conversation header and the right sidebar's top rows.
- Fixed the right sidebar's top rows having no blur or lighting on the Start page.
- Fixed the rounded corners on grouped headings in the model picker.

## 0.1.5-rc.2 (2026-09-21)

- First release.
- A Tone row under Appearance in Settings → General, with separate light and dark choices.
- Several tone skins: dark offers Space, Ember and Glade; light offers Frost, Sakura and Moss.
