# Changelog

English | [简体中文](CHANGELOG.zh-CN.md)

All notable changes to `dsh-desk-one` are documented here. The format follows Keep a Changelog and the version numbers mirror the official DSH release line.

## [0.2.2-rc.1] - 2026-11-06

* First release: one **Desk One** entry in the left sidebar hosting a tabbed panel; **Overview** is reserved as the future hub and stays intentionally blank in this release.
* Notes wall: add a note with the floating **＋** or by double-clicking empty space — the new sticky lands where you clicked, on top of the pile, and takes focus; the two floating actions sit at the bottom centre as frosted glass with the same top-left lighting as the themed composer; the empty wall shows a faint two-line hint. Write directly on the sticky, drag it by its body anywhere inside the visible wall (no scrollbars), click or drag a sticky to bring it to the front, resize it from the bottom-right corner, switch colors or drop it into the trash with the trash icon on hover; the sticky is flat square paper with deeper Notion-toned colors and matching deep hue swatches (no gradients), and text, position, size, layer and trash state persist through the official DSH storage domain. The trash, grouped with the **＋** button, restores or permanently deletes notes, and emptying it is an explicit two-step user action (never automatic).
* Task board: a fixed three-lane board (**To do / Doing / Done**) with colour-tagged cards whose title and description wrap automatically, day-granular due dates (overdue in red, due within a day with an amber outline), whole-card pointer dragging with a straight pointer-following ghost that flies back on invalid drops, double-click-to-add inside a lane, a collapsible Done lane, three view modes (manual / created / due), and an **Archive** for finished tasks that can be restored or deleted permanently (only Done cards can be archived). One-level groups per lane can be created, renamed, collapsed, reordered by dragging their grip, deleted, and cards can be dragged into or out of them. Narrow panels collapse the lanes into count-only columns instead of showing a horizontal scrollbar; inner scrollbars stay visually hidden. The `desk_task_*` agent tools read and write the same tasks.
* Capacity and clean-up: the wall is capped at **100** notes and the trash at **200**; the trash view renders 10 rows at a time with a total and a **Load more** button, and the trash button no longer carries a count badge. **Smash!** is a red frosted-glass icon button beside **＋** — always visible, two-step confirmation, and it sends the whole wall into the trash with a short fly-away animation; an armed Smash cancels when you click elsewhere or press **Esc**. The trash entry is separated from the wall actions by a hairline divider and its tooltip says **Open trash**; dropping a single sticky into the trash plays the same animation.
* Agent tools (`desk_note_*`) read and write the same notes, and the panel remembers the tab you were on when you come back.
