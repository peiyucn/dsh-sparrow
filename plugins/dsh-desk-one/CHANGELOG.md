# Changelog

All notable changes to `dsh-desk-one` are documented here. The format follows Keep a Changelog and the version numbers mirror the official DSH release line.

## [0.2.2-rc.1] - 2026-11-06

* First release: one **Desk One** entry in the left sidebar hosting a tabbed panel; Tabs are ordered Schedule / Notes / Tasks / Notebook / Sessions: **Schedule** leads, **Notebook** is reserved and stays blank in this release.
* Notes wall: add a note with **Capture** in the title-row actions or by double-clicking empty space — the new sticky lands where you clicked, on top of the pile, and takes focus; the notes wall is a fixed-width, vertically infinite canvas (the note stays under the pointer while dragging; the wall scrolls only by wheel/scrollbar, notes may live below the viewport, and double-clicking anywhere in the scrollport adds a note); the header is rebuilt to the conversation topbar spec: a compact 76px bar (solid by default; glass only under theme-tone) with title + one-line intro and view actions on the title row, official-style plain text tabs below plus a trailing ellipsis overflow launcher with a content-sized blurred menu (currently a coming-soon hint, reserved for future overflow tabs), a fixed 76px overlay with per-view offsets so first paint starts below it while scrolling content passes under the glass; the notes wall only shows a scrollbar when notes actually overflow, and the task board gets a 14px gap under the header; scrollable pages glide beneath the blur; the empty wall keeps a faint two-line hint. Write directly on the sticky, drag it by its body anywhere inside the visible wall (no scrollbars), click or drag a sticky to bring it to the front, resize it from the bottom-right corner (minimum 152×100 so the toolbar always fits), switch colors or drop it into the trash with the trash icon on hover, and each sticky shows its local creation date in low contrast at the bottom (hover for the full time); the sticky is flat square paper with deeper Notion-toned colors and matching deep hue swatches (no gradients), and text, position, size, layer and trash state persist through the official DSH storage domain. The trash entry in the command bar restores or permanently deletes notes, and emptying it is an explicit two-step user action (never automatic).
* Task board: a fixed three-lane board (**To do / Doing / Done**) with colour-tagged cards whose title and description wrap automatically (a stacked swatch opens a five-colour palette), day-granular due dates shown as a calendar icon plus the date (icon only when unset) (overdue in red, due within a day with an amber outline), whole-card pointer dragging (click a field to edit it, press anywhere else to drag) with a straight pointer-following ghost that flies back on invalid drops, plus lane/group highlighting and an insertion line while dragging, double-click-to-add inside a lane, a collapsible Done lane, a two-tier header with new task / a sort dropdown / archived tasks in its action row plus three view modes (manual / created / due), and an **Archived** view for finished tasks that can be restored or deleted permanently (only Done cards can be archived). Lane ordering is a single manual block sequence: ungrouped cards and group blocks can be interleaved in any order, and dragging a group between cards works like dragging a card. One-level groups are persisted records, not labels: they can be empty, renamed in place, collapsed, reordered by dragging their grip, deleted (their cards become ungrouped), and cards can be dragged into or out of them. Dragging a group moves the whole block like a card (the header and cards follow the pointer); dropping it on another lane moves its cards too, without a confirmation step. Groups in the Done lane can be archived as a unit: the archive view lists one row per group, restoring brings the group and its cards back, and deleting an archived group removes both. Cards can link one or more DSH sessions (and one session can serve several cards): chips carry their live state as colour — available sessions are green, archived/unavailable are neutral grey — and running ones get a slow orbiting green ring (static under reduced-motion); clicking a chip expands it in place up to the card width (the card height may grow, and a running chip keeps its animated border around the expanded box) showing last run, model time, turns, steps and token usage; click elsewhere restores it, and an arrow-only **Open session** action opens the session, and linking never overwrites the card's own text. The **@** entry sits on its own row under the linked chips; its picker is a page-level floating panel (no lane clipping) and lists the same session set as the composer's `@` menu, subagent sessions included. Narrow panels collapse the lanes into count-only columns instead of showing a horizontal scrollbar; an overflowing lane now shows a thin vertical scrollbar instead of squeezing its cards, and group headers use two rows (name + count) so they stay legible in narrow lanes. Lane titles are compact uppercase brand labels (bold with tracking); counts read as labels ("3 tasks") rather than bare numbers. Notes and the board share the same header and button style; object-level actions stay on their cards/lanes. The `desk_task_*` agent tools read and write the same tasks.
* **Schedule** tab: read-only aggregation of WeCom / iCloud CalDAV and any public ICS subscriptions; personal Outlook uses Graph device-code OAuth (multi-calendar, write scope requested) instead of a public ICS link. The top row splits equally between Today (split by a hairline: today's events, three colourful clickable counts for notes / unfinished tasks / active sessions, equally sized + Note / + Task / + Notebook white primary buttons with Notebook disabled) and Upcoming (a 1–14 day slider with the selected date range and its events); session deletion can copy the exact title with one click and no longer makes the list blink; calendar sources and sync moved into the shared top-bar actions, and the month / week / calendar calendar below (month by default, Monday-first with 5–6 rows as needed; week shows 8 hours per screen anchored on the current time and scrolls). An event opens a card near the click point with provider · account · calendar and closes on outside click / Esc; full connection and calendar management lives on a "Manage" sub-page, with one merged card per source (green dot = configured, grey = not; connection settings collapsed, calendars managed inside the card); opting a calendar out stops fetching and clears that calendar local mirror (useful for leftovers no longer visible in the original client). Syncs on open and every 15 minutes; credentials stay in the official credential store.
* **Sessions** tab: four sections (active / archived / stray / trash) share one row layout and icon actions; active uses a soft success outline while others stay neutral grey; parent/child sessions render as nested groups, actions live on the parent only and children move with it; active rows offer light archive only (no file changes), while trash/delete stay in the archived and trash sections; the archive section supports unarchive, move to trash and permanent delete (a parent takes its children with it); stray sessions are listed separately with archive / trash / delete; the trash supports expanding lineage, single or bulk restore, permanent delete, and a copyable path. Deleting a regular session requires typing its exact title, while blank sessions and bulk actions have their own confirmations. Every section loads 30 rows at a time; parents show "N children" and child rows are labelled "Child session". The standalone archive plugin's file-level management capabilities have moved here.
* Capacity and clean-up: the wall is capped at **100** notes and the trash at **200**; the trash view renders 10 rows at a time with a total and a **Load more** button, and the trash button no longer carries a count badge. **Smash!** is the red danger action in the command bar — always visible, two-step confirmation, and it sends the whole wall into the trash with a short fly-away animation; an armed Smash cancels when you click elsewhere or press **Esc**. The trash entry sits in the same command bar and its tooltip says **Open trash**; dropping a single sticky into the trash plays the same animation.
* Agent tools (`desk_note_*`) read and write the same notes, and the panel remembers the tab you were on when you come back.
